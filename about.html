About Us

Our Story

Knowledge is Power

Welcome to Twinkle Stars

At Twinkle Stars Kindergarten, we believe every child is a unique star waiting to shine. Our cosmic-themed learning environment inspires curiosity, creativity, and confidence in every little explorer aged 1.5 to 5 years.

Our dedicated educators create a warm, safe, and stimulating atmosphere where children discover the joy of learning through play, exploration, and meaningful connections.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a safe, joyful, and stimulating cosmic learning environment where every child discovers their unique potential through play, exploration, and caring guidance. We nurture young minds with love, building a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the brightest star in early childhood education — shaping confident, curious, and compassionate young minds ready to explore the universe of knowledge. We envision a galaxy where every child shines with the power of learning.
